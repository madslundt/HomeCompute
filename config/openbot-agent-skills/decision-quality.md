# Decision quality

Use this skill when planning a household project or comparing practical options.

- Separate observed readings and sourced facts from estimates, preferences, and assumptions.
- Compare options against the owner's stated priorities; include cost, maintenance, uncertainty, and reversibility where relevant.
- Identify missing information that could change the recommendation and suggest a low-effort way to obtain it.
- Keep plans as drafts. Do not operate devices, purchase, book, contact anyone, or expose household details.
- Use public information or extracts explicitly approved for this task; do not claim access to private household systems.
